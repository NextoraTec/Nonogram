## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I play Nonogram?
Use the number clues on each row and column to determine which cells should be filled in. Complete the grid to reveal the hidden picture.

### Game Progress and Cloud Save
If you experience problems with game progress or cloud synchronization, please contact us.

### Account and Login Issues
If you cannot sign in or access your account, please contact our support team.

### Report a Bug
If you encounter a bug, crash, or unexpected behavior, please send us a description of the issue.
