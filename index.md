# NonoGram Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 26, 2026  
**Draft status:** Complete the developer contact details below before publishing.

## English

### Overview

NonoGram is an offline nonogram puzzle game. We respect your privacy. NonoGram does not require an account and does not collect, upload, or sell your personal information through the app.

### How the app handles information

Puzzle progress, level records, best times, and a small amount of locally cached information used to restore Pro access are stored on your device using iOS local storage. The app does not send this information to us or to a third-party server.

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not use advertising identifiers to track you.

### App Store subscriptions

If you purchase a NonoGram Pro subscription, Apple processes your payment, billing information, and subscription management through the App Store. We do not receive or store your full payment details. The app uses Apple’s StoreKit service to obtain the transaction and entitlement information needed to determine whether Pro features are available.

Apple’s handling of this information is governed by Apple’s privacy policy and terms. See [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Sharing and tracking

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected by the app. The app does not collect such information and does not track your activity across other companies’ apps or websites.

Apple’s operating system or the App Store may process information needed for transactions, diagnostics, or service operation according to your device settings and Apple’s policies. Those practices are controlled by Apple, not by the NonoGram developer.

### Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device. Deleting NonoGram removes the local data stored by the app from the device. Whether that data is included in a device backup depends on Apple’s system settings and backup behavior.

App Store subscriptions and purchase records are managed by Apple and are not deleted from your Apple Account when you remove the app. You can manage subscriptions or make account-data requests through Apple.

### Children’s privacy

NonoGram does not ask users to provide personal information and is not intended to collect children’s personal information through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details below. The app does not provide an account or upload feature for submitting such information.

### Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if this policy changes. If the app’s data practices change in the future, we will update this policy and applicable privacy disclosures accordingly.
